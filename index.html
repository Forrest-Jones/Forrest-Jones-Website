About me

I am a senior capital markets executive with 30+ years driving investor relations, Reg D 506(b)/506(c) capital raises, and revenue operations across oil & gas, real estate, private equity, and government-backed contracts. I am personally responsible for $10B+ in capital raised: $9B in the public markets and $1B in private equity and venture capital, spread over 40 companies, some now public and some still private.

Today I serve as Chief Investment Officer across the Blackwater group — Blackwater Investment Group, Blackwater Evaluations Group, and Blackwater Forensics — and as founder and Chief Executive Officer of Proverbs 1821 Consulting LLC, the umbrella firm my advisory work runs through and a SAM.gov-registered federal contractor that teams with specialist trades to win government work. Alongside that I hold the Chief Financial Officer seat at five active portfolio companies: Genvor, The Oasis of Texas, US Standard Capital, Solomon Energy of Texas, and Colossus Strategic Holdings — continuous financial leadership across multi-industry capital structures, with the flexibility to engage fractionally when the contract calls for it.

As a Certified Hedge Fund Professional, I bring fund-manager discipline to structuring, LP/GP relations, and capital allocation. And because I have been a classically trained programmer since 1996 — with a 2022 AI Software Engineering degree from The Flatiron School in New York — I build the systems that run the raise rather than waiting on a vendor to do it.

That means Claude integrations, GoHighLevel automation, and Meta Ads deployed at scale to compress the sales cycle and accelerate accredited investor acquisition. Equally credible across the deal table, the dialer, and the codebase — and I handle the conversation in English, Mandarin, or Spanish.
