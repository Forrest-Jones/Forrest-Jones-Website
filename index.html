About

About me

I spent two decades in capital markets — equities, commodities, forex, futures, alternatives — raising capital, managing risk, and living with the consequences of both. Then I went and got credentialed as an engineer at Flatiron School in New York and moved into technology full time.

That combination is the whole point. Most engineers have to be told what a reconciliation break is. Most finance people have to be told why the schema matters. I have done both jobs, so the translation layer between them is free.

Today most of my work is AI: agents, LLM-powered products, and the retrieval and evaluation plumbing that makes them trustworthy enough to put in front of clients and capital. Underneath, it is still the same craft — clean data models, honest APIs, tested code, and a scope that ships.

I have been in crypto since 2011, I hold the Certified Hedge Fund Professional designation, and I still write the whitepaper myself. If you are building something at the intersection of money and machine intelligence, we will get along.
